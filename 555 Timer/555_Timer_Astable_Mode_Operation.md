The charging time of capacitor to a value 0.67*V<sub>cc</sub> is given as:

$$
t_{c} = 0.69(R_1 + R_2)C_1
$$

This time $t_{c}$ is also the time during which the output is On ($t_{on}$). Similarly, the discharging time of the capacitor is given as:

$$
t_{d} = 0.69(R_2)C_1
$$

This time $t_{d}$ is the time during which the output is Off ($t_{off}$).
